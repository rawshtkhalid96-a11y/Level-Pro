Install GeoLevel Pro on Any Device

Works natively on Android, iOS (iPhone/iPad), and Windows/Mac PCs

One-Click Installation Available
Install directly onto your device home screen / desktop right now.
Android
  1. Open the app link in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (Three Dots) menu at top-right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Launch anytime from your app drawer—even offline in the field!
iOS (iPhone/iPad)
  1. Open the app link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow) at bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" to run full-screen like a native iOS app.
Windows / Mac PC
  1. Open the link in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the Install Icon () on the right side of the URL address bar.
  3. Click "Install" to create a standalone desktop app window.
